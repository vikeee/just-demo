Our mission is to put Indian inspired niche perfumery on the map through an uncompromising attitude towards quality and an obsession to represent the new India, one that is modern, youthful, vibrant and bold.

About Rahasya

Chapter One

An homage to the neighbourhood Indian bookstore, where rich scents of aged books and fresh ink create a unique atmosphere. Infused with sandalwood, leather, tobacco, cardamom and delicate floral notes, this fragrance captures the charm of these analog havens.

Discover

Cutting Rain

As India’s summer heat peaks, anticipation builds for the first monsoon rains, symbolizing renewal and celebration. Evoking this joy, our fragrance blends saffron, grapefruit, rose, marigold, sandalwood, and tea, capturing the refreshing spirit of rain-soaked verandahs and warm chai with loved ones.

Discover

Oud Mangifera

For many in India, mangoes are synonymous with lazy summer days. We aim to capture the richness of the royal mango, and pay tribute to its role as the king of fruits through a unique blend of davana, mango, geranium, blackcurrant, rose, leather, incense and oud.

Discover

Love Marriage

Ah, the Indian wedding. Extravagant decorations, delectable treats, boozy festivities, and a union of two families - an Indian wedding is a spectacle like no other. To recreate an atmosphere of love and passion, we blended notes of pistachio, praline, saffron, rum, marigold, and sandalwood.

Discover

JOIN THE COMMUNITY

We're here to represent the modern Indian identity through fine fragrance. Along the way, we hope to celebrate new age music, art and culture that is being pushed by talented Indian creators and world class brands. India is quickly redefining boundaries, and breaking stereotypes. We couldn’t resist being part of this movement, and we want you to join us on this journey.
